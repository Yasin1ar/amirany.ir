# Hello!

I'm Yasin, and this is my blog.

I made it hoping it'll motivate me to write more consistently—to share what I learn, speak my mind, and maybe experience a tiny piece of the classic golden era of blogging, especially now that we're living through the rise of AI.

People say writing helps you think. I hope that's true. Maybe this blog will make me less lazy, encourage me to organize my thoughts, and even help me wire my neurons a little better (thanks to the Feynman Technique).

I plan to write mostly about things I'm learning, so expect posts about *coding*, *algorithms*, *backend development*, and other computer-related topics. But I also love *movies*, *music*, and *football* (Barcelona ❤️), so those might show up from time to time.

This isn't a dedicated programming blog where you come every day to learn computer science from an expert—I'm definitely not one. I'm still learning myself. And it isn't a daily journal either... although, now that I think about it, maybe that wouldn't be such a bad idea.

Honestly, I don't know what this blog will eventually become, and I'm okay with that. Maybe it'll stay small. Maybe it'll slowly turn into something I'm proud of.

I'm just an ordinary guy with an ordinary blog, writing ordinary posts.

You're **welcome** here anyway.
